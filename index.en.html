JobWatch

Watch an area on a job board. Get a notification when the postings change. Everything stays on your device. · 中文
Get it on the Edge Add-ons store The Chrome Web Store listing is in preparation (needs an overseas payment profile). The Edge build has the same features.

Privacy policy

The full text is here: Privacy Policy.
No server, no account, no upload — watch items and change history live in your own browser.

How to use it

  • Install, open a job board page, click the toolbar icon → "Watch an area on this page"
  • Hover the job list, click the highlighted area, give it a name and pick an interval
  • Next time you open that page, any text change triggers a notification and a history entry

Where it works (please read)

Supported: company career sites and self-hosted job pages (including hosted boards such as Greenhouse or Lever, at your own judgement).
Not supported: LinkedIn and Indeed — their user agreements prohibit third-party extensions from reading or modifying their pages. Please do not use it there.
