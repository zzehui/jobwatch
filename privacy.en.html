Privacy Policy

JobWatch (Chrome extension) · Version 0.3.0 · Effective 2026-11-09 · 中文
In one sentence: this extension has no server. The page content you select and the change history it records stay in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded, shared, or sold.

1. What the extension does

You pick an area on a job board page (for example the whole job list). The extension remembers it. When you open that page later, it checks whether the text in that area has changed. If it has, you get a browser notification and a new entry in the extension's change history.

2. What data is stored

Everything below is stored only on your device (chrome.storage.local) and is produced by your own actions:

  • Watch items: page address, how the selected area is located (CSS selector), the name you gave it, check interval
  • The text snapshot from the last check, or its hash (used to decide whether anything changed)
  • Change history: timestamp and a short summary of the change
  • Settings: notification on/off, intervals, and similar preferences

Not collected: browsing history, accounts, passwords, cookies, contacts, location, device identifiers. No profiling, no advertising, no credit scoring.

3. Where the data goes

Nowhere. The extension contains no backend and makes no network request to send your data off your device. The only network access is reading the area you selected on a page you granted permission for (see section 4).

4. Why each permission is needed

  • storage: keep watch items, snapshots and history
  • alarms: run the check on the interval you choose (15/30/60 minutes)
  • notifications: show a system notification when a change is detected
  • scripting + activeTab: read the text of the area you selected on the page you have open
  • Optional host permissions (http://*/*, https://*/*): requested for a specific site only when you click "watch", so background checks can run later. You can revoke this at any time in Chrome's extension settings.

5. Deleting your data

  • Delete a single watch item, or clear the change history, inside the extension
  • Uninstall the extension — Chrome removes its local data with it
  • There is no server-side copy, so deletion is permanent

6. Third parties

No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. No data is transferred to or shared with third parties.

7. Supported sites

Use the extension on company career sites and self-hosted job pages — sites that allow automated access. LinkedIn, Indeed and similar platforms prohibit third-party extensions from reading or modifying their pages in their user agreements; do not use this extension on those sites. The same note appears in the store listing.

8. Children

This extension is intended for job seekers, not for children under 13, and does not knowingly process children's data.

9. Changes to this policy

If data handling changes, this page is updated and the effective date above is revised. Significant changes are noted in the extension's release notes.
